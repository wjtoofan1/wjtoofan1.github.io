Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

The short version: Khazna keeps your inventory on your device. There's no account to create, no ads, no tracking, and we don't sell or share your data. The only outside service involved is the one that confirms your Khazna Pro purchase.

This policy explains how NovaStack Labs LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the Khazna app for iOS and Android.

Information that stays on your device

The items you record (names, categories, values, purchase dates, rooms, serial numbers, notes), and the photos and receipts you add, are stored only in Khazna's private storage on your device. We never receive them and have no way to see them.

  • Camera and photos: Khazna asks for access only when you choose to add a photo. The pictures you take or pick are copied into the app. Khazna doesn't read the rest of your photo library.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint: the optional app lock uses your device's built-in authentication. Your biometric data never leaves the device's secure hardware and is never shared with us.
  • Backups, PDF reports and CSV exports are created only when you ask. You decide where they are saved or sent (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email), and that service's own privacy policy then applies.

Purchases

Khazna Pro is sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play, which handle all payments. We never see your card or payment details. To check whether your Pro purchase is active (including renewals, refunds and restores on a new device), Khazna uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID that isn't linked to your name, your purchase receipts and history, basic device information (such as operating system, app version and store country) and your IP address. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No sale or rental of personal information.
  • No analytics on the contents of your vault.

Your choices and deleting data

You can edit or delete any item in the app at any time. Uninstalling Khazna removes everything stored by the app on that device, so create a backup first if you want to keep your inventory. To ask RevenueCat to delete purchase records linked to your app user ID, email us at the address below.

Children

Khazna isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to processing. Because your inventory never reaches us, these mostly concern the purchase records described above. Contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

معلومات تبقى على جهازك

القطع التي تسجلها (الأسماء والفئات والقيم وتواريخ الشراء والغرف والأرقام التسلسلية والملاحظات)، والصور والفواتير التي تضيفها، تُحفظ فقط في مساحة التخزين الخاصة بالتطبيق على جهازك. لا تصلنا هذه البيانات ولا يمكننا الاطلاع عليها.

  • الكاميرا والصور: يطلب التطبيق الإذن فقط عندما تختار إضافة صورة، وتُنسخ الصور التي تلتقطها أو تختارها إلى التطبيق. لا يقرأ التطبيق بقية مكتبة صورك.
  • Face ID وTouch ID وبصمة الإصبع: يستخدم قفل التطبيق الاختياري نظام المصادقة المدمج في جهازك. لا تغادر بياناتك الحيوية الجهاز ولا تُشارك معنا.
  • النسخ الاحتياطية وتقارير PDF وملفات CSV تُنشأ فقط عندما تطلب ذلك، وأنت من يقرر أين تُحفظ أو تُرسل، وتنطبق حينها سياسة خصوصية تلك الخدمة.
